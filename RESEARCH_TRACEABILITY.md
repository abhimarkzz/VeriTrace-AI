## 2. Theoretical Principles Directly Realized in Architecture

1. **Classification Alone Is Insufficient**:
   A pure text-in/label-out classifier cannot distinguish between genuine facts and plausible-sounding rumors. VeriTrace requires an explicit evidence retrieval step before a verdict is formed.
2. **Uncertainty Is an Outcome, Not a Failure**:
   When public records lack authoritative confirmation, forcing a binary classification leads to catastrophic hallucination. VeriTrace routes claims into `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE` whenever evidence is absent or disputed.
3. **Decoupled Verification Pipeline**:
   The entity that extracts the claim is separated from the entity that searches for evidence, which is in turn separated from the entity that evaluates entailment. This modularity prevents compounding model errors.
